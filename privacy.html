Privacy Policy

Last updated: 2026-11-23

Vector OpenClaw is a private application used by a single person to let their own AI assistant work with their own Google account.

Data accessed

With the account owner's consent, the application accesses Gmail, Google Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Contacts and Tasks data of that account only.

How data is used

Data is used solely to perform tasks requested by the account owner (reading, organizing and drafting email, working with documents, managing calendar events and tasks). Data is processed on the owner's own computer and may be sent to the AI model provider chosen by the owner to complete a requested task.

Sharing

Data is not sold, not used for advertising, and not shared with third parties other than as needed to complete the owner's own requests.

Google API Services User Data Policy

Use of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements.

Retention and deletion

Access can be revoked at any time at https://myaccount.google.com/permissions. Locally stored tokens are deleted when access is removed.
