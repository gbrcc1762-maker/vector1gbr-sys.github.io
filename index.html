Vector OpenClaw

Vector OpenClaw is a private, single-user personal assistant (an OpenClaw agent) that runs on its owner's own computer. It uses Google APIs (Gmail, Drive, Docs, Sheets, Calendar, Contacts, Tasks) only for its own Google account, at the request of its owner.

It is not a public service and does not accept new users.
